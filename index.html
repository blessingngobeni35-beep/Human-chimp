🧬 Human & Chimpanzee Similarities

What do we have in common?

🧬 Shared Evolutionary History

Humans and chimpanzees are different species, but they share an ancient common ancestor.

💡 Remember: Humans did not evolve from the chimpanzees living today.

🧠 Brain & Learning

Both humans and chimpanzees have complex brains and can learn from experience.

🤝 Social Behavior

Both species live in social groups and can form relationships with other members of their groups.

🛠️ Tool Use

Chimpanzees can use objects as tools, while humans have developed extremely complex technologies.

🧬 DNA

Humans and chimpanzees have very similar DNA because of their shared evolutionary history.

💡 DNA comparisons are one of the ways scientists study evolutionary relationships.

🔍 Human & Chimpanzee Differences

How are we different?

🚶 Movement

Humans are adapted for regular upright walking on two legs. Chimpanzees usually move using all four limbs, although they can walk upright for short periods.

🧠 Brain

Humans generally have a larger brain relative to body size than chimpanzees, with major differences in brain organization and development.

🗣️ Communication

Humans use highly complex spoken and written languages. Chimpanzees communicate using vocalizations, gestures, facial expressions and other signals.

🏠 Culture & Technology

Humans create extremely complex technologies, cities and institutions. Chimpanzees also show learned behaviors and traditions, including different tool-use behaviors between groups.

💡 Important

Humans and chimpanzees are different species. Their differences developed over a very long evolutionary history after their lineages separated from a common ancestor.

🌳 Evolution

The evolutionary relationship between humans and chimpanzees

🧬 A Common Ancestor

Humans and chimpanzees are different species. However, scientists have evidence that their evolutionary lineages share an ancient common ancestor.

💡 Important: Humans did not evolve from the chimpanzees that are alive today.

🌿 Think of a Tree

Evolution can be imagined like a branching tree. A branch can split into different lineages that continue changing over many generations.

🦴 How Do Scientists Know?

Scientists compare fossils, anatomy, DNA and other evidence to investigate evolutionary relationships.

🧠 What Does Evolution Mean?

Evolution is the change in inherited characteristics of populations over generations.

🧠 Behavior & Intelligence

How humans and chimpanzees learn, communicate and live together

👥 Social Groups

Humans and chimpanzees are social species. Chimpanzees live in communities with complex relationships, while humans live in many different kinds of social groups.

🗣️ Communication

Humans communicate using complex languages, gestures and facial expressions. Chimpanzees also use vocalizations, gestures, facial expressions and body movements to communicate.

🛠️ Tool Use

Chimpanzees can use objects as tools, such as using suitable objects to help obtain food. Humans have developed much more complex tools and technologies.

📚 Learning

Chimpanzees can learn through observation and experience. Some behaviors can also be passed between members of a group.

💡 Important

Intelligence is not one simple ability. Scientists study many different abilities, including memory, learning, problem-solving and communication.

🤝 Human–Chimpanzee Interaction

How humans and chimpanzees share the world

🐒 Chimpanzees and Their Environment

Chimpanzees depend on forests for food, shelter and places to live. Human activities can change these environments.

💡 Protecting natural habitats is an important part of chimpanzee conservation.

🌳 Habitat Loss

Activities such as deforestation and changes in land use can reduce and divide chimpanzee habitats.

When forests become separated, chimpanzee communities can have less access to food and safe areas.

🔬 Scientific Research

Scientists study chimpanzees to learn about their behavior, communication, social relationships and ecology.

🧠 Researchers should follow ethical guidelines and avoid unnecessarily disturbing the animals.

🌍 Conservation

Conservation can include protecting forests, monitoring chimpanzee populations and working with communities that live near chimpanzee habitats.
